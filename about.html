Software Engineering Laboratory Project Overview

1. Executive Summary & Objective

The Civil Services Officer Posting & Transfer Optimization System is a web application designed to systematically manage civil services officer transfer applications, vacancy allocations, document verification, policy compliance, optimization scoring, committee review decisions, and official transfer order generation.

3. Rule-Based Optimization Formula (100 Points)

Score = Eligibility (25) + Service Duration (20) + Location Preference (20) + Vacancy (15) + Admin Priority (10) + Transfer History (10)

  • Eligibility (25 pts): Meets active policy minimum service years and verified document status.
  • Service Duration (20 pts): Score scales up to 20 points for 5+ years in current posting.
  • Location Preference (20 pts): Matches officer's preferred location against available vacancies.
  • Vacancy Availability (15 pts): Verified available position count at preferred station.
  • Administrative Priority (10 pts): Priority bonus for medical, spouse co-location, or special hardship cases.
  • Transfer History (10 pts): Seniority bonus for time elapsed since previous transfer.
