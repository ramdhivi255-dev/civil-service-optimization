End-to-End Governance Workflow

Systematic 6-step lifecycle from request submission to transfer completion

Step 01
Submit Transfer Request

Officer submits location preferences & supporting documents.

Step 02
Document Audit & Verification

Cadre Administrator verifies supporting documents and policy rules.

Step 03
Optimization & Ranking

System calculates 100-point score & assigns HIGH/MEDIUM/LOW priority.

Step 04
Committee Review & Decision

Transfer Committee evaluates candidate dossier and approves/rejects.

Step 05
Order Generation & Notification

System issues official Transfer Order & notifies the officer.

Step 06
Acknowledgement & Completion

Officer acknowledges order, confirms joining, and updates posting.
